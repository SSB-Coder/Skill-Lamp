# Skill Lamp — Live Demo Script: 3-Act Persona Flow

> **Target Duration:** 2:30 – 3:00 Minutes | **Format:** Stopwatch-paced live stage demo with synchronized UI actions and direct, confident, judge-facing narration.

---

## Stage & Setup Checklist
* **Display Resolution:** 1080p / 16:9 fullscreen browser
* **Starting State:** Skill Lamp Landing / Persona Selection Screen
* **Demo Preset:** Pre-loaded candidate data & pre-warmed Databricks Genie Space session

---

## Live Demo Flow & Narration

### Beat 1 — Hook & the Institutional Problem `[0:00 – 0:25]`

* **Visual / Stage Setup:** Stand center stage, screen showing the clean Skill Lamp entry portal.
* **Stage Action:** Point to the dual-persona interface on screen.

> **Spoken Narration:**
> *"Every placement season, university placement cells face a double crisis: TPOs drown in manual spreadsheets and recruiter emails, while students receive generic advice like 'learn more skills' with zero visibility into actual ROI."*
> 
> *"Meet Skill Lamp — the governed placement intelligence assistant built on Databricks Unity Catalog and Genie Spaces."*

---

### Beat 2 — Act I: TPO Portal (Live Genie Copilot, JD Matcher & Candidate Spreadsheet) `[0:25 – 1:15]`

* **UI Action 1:** Click **"Demo TPO Officer"** — candidate table opens instantly pre-loaded with data.

> **Spoken Narration (Part 1):**
> *"Here is the TPO Command Portal. The placement officer has the complete candidate roster. When a recruiter emails a raw JD, the TPO clicks 'Paste Recruiter JD,' drops in the description, and Genie instantly translates unstructured text into governed SQL."*

* **UI Action 2:** Paste a sample recruiter JD (or click a quick chip) — table dynamically filters eligible candidates. Expand the **SQL Trace Drawer** and point to the **Lineage Strip**.

> **Spoken Narration (Part 2):**
> *"Genie parses the requirements and filters the exact eligible candidates. Notice the Lineage Strip: Unity Catalog enforces column-level PII masking before any query runs. The TPO clicks 'Export Shortlist' for the recruiter."*

---

### Beat 3 — Act II: Student Time Machine (Target Reverse Roadmap & Live Skill Simulation) `[1:15 – 2:05]`

* **UI Action 1:** Click the header pill `[ Switch Persona ⇄ Student: Priya ]` for an instantaneous persona transition.

> **Spoken Narration (Part 1):**
> *"Now let's switch to Priya Nair, an ISE student. Notice the strict privacy boundary: Priya can only see her own profile. Currently, she has a 40% placement probability and qualifies for 3 companies."*

* **UI Action 2:** In the **Reverse Roadmap**, select **Databricks (48.0 LPA)** and click **"Simulate Skill Bridge."**

> **Spoken Narration (Part 2):**
> *"Priya targets Databricks. Skill Lamp detects she's missing Databricks Data Engineering. She clicks Simulate: live, in real time, Skill Lamp queries 6 years of historical cohort data. Her placement probability jumps from 40% to 80% (+40 pts), her expected package climbs by +10.30 LPA, and our Synergy Engine shows how pairing it with PySpark boosts her to 92%."*

---

### Beat 4 — Act III: Architecture, Governance & Closing `[2:05 – 2:30]`

* **Visual Cue:** Highlight the bottom system architecture & governance badge (Unity Catalog + Genie + React). Make direct eye contact with the judging panel.

> **Spoken Narration:**
> *"Skill Lamp combines Unity Catalog data governance, Genie's semantic AI, deterministic Python arithmetic, and a responsive React UI into one enterprise platform. It turns static placement history into actionable student destiny. Thank you!"*
